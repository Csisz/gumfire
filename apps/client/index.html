About GUMFIRE

A turn-based artillery game for two to four players: gummy Gumlings battle with pepper rockets, popcorn bombs and rolling pins across a frozen snack factory, a garden picnic, a toy desk, a garage and a bathroom — hot-seat on one keyboard, against the CPU, or online.

Controls — ← → walk · ↑ ↓ aim · Enter jump · Space hold to charge, release to fire · Tab weapons · 1–5 fuse · H all keys in a match.

Made by Viktor. Painted art generated with Higgsfield and cut out for the game; music and sounds are made live in your browser; built with Claude.
