# M7 — Turn system and teams

Status: **done** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
A real match: two (up to six) teams take turns on one keyboard, with turn and retreat timers,
settle-then-reveal between turns, deaths, sudden death, and a correct win / draw every time.

## Checklist
- [x] `match/ruleset.ts`: `RulesetJson` (seconds, px) → `Ruleset` (ticks) with plan defaults
      (45 s turn, 3 s retreat, 15 min round, sudden death `both` with +20 px water per turn,
      100 hp, 4 per team, 40 px spacing, 60 px above water) and validation errors
- [x] `match/match.ts`: teams (2..6, 1..8 characters), random placement on dry, roomy ground
      with the `mapgen` stream (round-robin across teams, relaxed fallbacks), manual spawns for
      tests, seeded turn order, `livingTeams`
- [x] `turn/turn.ts`: phases `turnPrep → turnActive → retreat → settling → damageReveal →
      (turn end) → turnPrep | matchOver`; control only for the active character in
      turnActive/retreat; a turn-ending shot starts the retreat (no second shot, no weapon
      change); control ends on timeout, damage, water, retreat end or End Turn; settling waits
      10 quiet ticks (20 s cap); reveal hold; death blasts loop back into settling
- [x] Rotation: next living team in order, next living character in the team's roster
      (`characterSelect: free` + `nextCharacter` command switches before the first shot);
      wind rerolled every turn
- [x] Round clock counts only during turns and retreats; at zero: sudden death `hpToOne`,
      `water`, `both` (water rises every turn prep, bodies woken) or `roundEnds` (most hp wins)
- [x] Victory: last team with a living character; everybody dead in one resolution = draw
- [x] GameState schema 7 (`match`), match state in the hash; `debugSelect` is ignored in a
      match; free play (sandbox tools, old tests) is unchanged
- [x] Sandbox: Mode selector (Free play / Hot-seat match 2 × 4), turn banner with timers and
      round clock, team hp totals, win/draw line, Backspace = End Turn, R = rematch, rising water
      drawn
