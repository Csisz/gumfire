# M3 — Physics & collision

Status: **done** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
Deterministic disc bodies moving over the terrain bitmap: gravity, collision, bounce, friction,
rest, wake-up after terrain changes, water, map bounds. Replaces the M0 demo bouncers.

## Checklist

      (negated sum of solid offsets, ×256 unit vector), support test
- [x] `physics/body.ts`: `Body` (subpixel pos/vel, radius, restitution, friction, gravity
      scale, sleep, still ticks, drown ticks); `stepBodies`:

## Performance (bundled)
60 bodies thrown into the arena: mean 86–192 µs, p95 150–329 µs per tick while ≥ 30 are awake (varies run to run on the shared container CPU)
(budget 500 µs). Sleeping bodies cost nothing.

## Automated tests (98)
Ring/disc shapes, normals on floor and wall, exact free fall, terminal velocity, landing without
penetration and falling asleep at the exact surface pixel, bouncing, 1 px wall at 4/8/16/32 px
per tick, sliding off a 45° slope, resting on a ~10° slope, 40 thrown bodies never inside
terrain for 600 ticks, wake-and-fall after a carve, girder pops a buried body out, drowning
timing, lost off-map, spawn validation, a settled scene stays bit-identical.

## Manual test (headless Chromium)
Drops, a slingshot throw, carving under resting Gumlings, 50-Gumling rain on both maps. Bodies
settle in valleys, on platforms and in craters; none rest on steep slopes; the pit drowns them.
Verify passes after each scenario, including after a map switch.

## Known limitations / next
- Bodies do not collide with each other (classic characters also pass through each other when
  walking; body-to-body knocks are an optional ruleset feature for later).
- Next, M4 — character movement: `CharacterController` (walk with ±6 px step, jump, backflip,
  falling, fall damage, drowning) on top of `Body`, driven by the input frame.
