# M5 — Aim, projectile, wind

Status: **done** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
Charged, aimed shots with the first data-driven weapon (Pepper Rocket), ballistic projectiles
affected by wind, impacts on terrain and characters, and a wind HUD.

## Checklist
- [x] `weapons/definition.ts`: `WeaponJson` (authored, ballistic subset) → `WeaponDef` (sim
      units) with validation and clear errors; `launchSpeed`; ADR-004
- [x] `packages/content`: `weapons/pepper_rocket.json`, `WEAPONS`; compiled into the state at
      match creation, `weaponsHash` in the state hash
- [x] Character `charging` state: Fire pressed → charge 1 tick per tick; release fires with the
      charge; full charge fires by itself; no walking while charging; losing the ground drops
      the shot. `selectWeapon` command.
- [x] `weapons/projectile.ts`: launch vector from aim + facing, muzzle offset, exact ≤ 1 px
      substeps, gravity × gravityScale, wind × windFactor with an exact remainder accumulator,
      impact on terrain or characters (shooter ignored for 6 ticks), point-blank into a wall
      explodes at once, water splash (no explosion), lost off-map, max-life force trigger
- [x] Explosion (M5 scope): carves a crater of the payload radius, wakes bodies, events
      `Exploded` / `TerrainChanged(cause: explosion)`. Damage and knockback come at M6.
- [x] `environment/wind.ts`: W ∈ [−100, 100], ±0.06 px/tick² at full; `rollWind` = previous ±
      two uniform(−20, 20) draws (triangular, ≤ ±40 per turn); commands `debugSetWind`,
      `debugRollWind`
- [x] GameState schema 5 (`weapons`, `weaponsHash`, `projectiles`, `nextProjectileId`, `wind`);
      golden scenario now fires rockets and rerolls the wind
- [x] Sandbox: Space charge/fire with a power meter along the aim, pepper-rocket sprite with a
      trail, sugar-crystal explosion, cocoa splash, wind gauge (X = new wind), camera follows
      the rocket then the blast
