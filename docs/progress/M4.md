# M4 — Character movement

Status: **done** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
The first controllable Gumling: deliberate pixel walking, jumps, backflips, falls with fall
damage, drowning — driven by recorded 16-bit input frames, fully deterministic.

## Checklist
- [x] `character/character.ts`: `Character` (id, team, body, state, facing, aim, hp,
      fall immunity), states `idle / walk / jumpPrep / air / landing / drowning / dead`
- [x] Walking (plan §10.2): 1 px per tick (50 px/s); each step searches the next column from
      6 px up to 6 px down for a free, supported spot; blocked by walls; walks off ledges into
      a short fall. Round-body geometry lets it roll over vertical steps up to its radius
      (9 px); 10 px and higher block it (tested).
- [x] Jumps: Enter → 10-tick crouch → forward jump (2.5, −4.5 px/tick); a second Enter inside
      the crouch → backflip (−1.1, −7.2 px/tick, apex ≈ 130 px, keeps facing)
- [x] Airborne = physics body (`stepBody`, now returning contact/impact info); lands on
      surfaces within 60° of up, slides off steeper ones; 8-tick landing recovery
- [x] Fall damage from the normal impact speed; placement is fall-immune (the same flag will
      carry blast immunity at M6)
- [x] Aim ↑/↓: 1.5°/tick, 3°/tick after 12 ticks, clamped ±90°
- [x] Terrain edits: a crater under a character makes it fall; a girder on it pops it out on top
- [x] Water: drowning → dead after 75 ticks; walking off the map edge is lethal
- [x] Commands `debugSpawnCharacter`, `debugSelect`; only the active character gets input;
      dead characters cannot be selected
- [x] GameState schema 4 (`characters`, `nextCharacterId`, `activeCharacter`); golden scenario
      now includes a controlled character (walks, jumps both ways, aims)
- [x] Sandbox: C = place Gumling (team colours in order), ←/→ walk, ↑/↓ aim, Enter jump,
      Enter×2 backflip, Tab next Gumling, follow camera (L), aim reticle, HP label, active
      marker, squash on crouch/landing, damage pop-ups, last-jump readout
- [x] Input edges: every key press is delivered to the sim as its own edge — a tap shorter than
      a tick is not lost, and two presses between the same ticks get a released tick between
      them (verified: double-tap with a 30 ms gap → backflip, 3/3)

## Tuning changes vs the plan (logged in `docs/tuning.md`)
| Parameter | Plan | Now | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fall damage threshold | 5.5 px/tick | 7.5 px/tick | a backflip lands at 7.2 px/tick and must not hurt on flat ground |
| Fall damage slope | ×8 | ×10 | terminal speed deals 45; a 200 px ledge ≈ 14 |
| Jump crouch | 6 ticks | 10 ticks | a comfortable double-tap window (200 ms) for the backflip |

## Automated tests (118)
Spawn/land without damage, fall immunity on placement, exact 1 px/tick walking and facing,
6 px step climbed / 10 px step blocked, 200 px wall blocks, both-directions input, input only
to the active character, ledge fall damage equals the formula, small drops harmless, formula
values, backflip on flat ground harmless, forward jump distance, backflip height ≥ 120 px and
direction, jump into a wall never embeds, aim steps/acceleration/clamp, crater under character,
girder pop-out, drowning and dead-selection rules, 70° ramp slide-off, character determinism
(replay + clone), plus all previous tests.
