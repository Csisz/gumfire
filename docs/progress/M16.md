## Checklist
- [x] **Alliances** in the sim: `TeamConfig.side` / `TeamState.side` (GameState schema 13,
      hashed). Sides take turns (A, B, A, B…; each side's teams in their shuffled order); a
      side wins when it is the last one standing ("Mint & Grape win!"); sudden death "most hp
      wins" counts per side. Free-for-all is unchanged (same winners and end ticks).
- [x] Match setup: every slot has a side (A–D) next to player / CPU; one side only cannot
      start. The team list shows side badges; awards count allies as teammates; damage to an
      ally is not credited.
- [x] CPUs treat allies as friends (never prefer a shot that hurts them).
- [x] **Series**: single match, best of 3 or best of 5. The results show the score per side
      (● ○), "Next match (n)" plays on a fresh map, the winner of the series gets the title,
      and a series MVP team (KOs, then damage over the series). Draws do not count; a series
      always ends.
- [x] **Replays**: "Save replay" writes the match as a small JSON file (config + run-length
      coded inputs + commands + looks; a handmade map's materials run-length coded). "Watch a
      replay" on the title screen opens one; "Watch replay" on the results plays the match
      just finished. The viewer: play / pause (Space), previous / next turn (← →), from the
      start, 1×–8× speed, progress, Esc to leave. It checks the recording reproduces the saved
      final state and says so if it does not.
- [x] **Replay last turn** in the in-game menu: the turn so far, then back to the match.
