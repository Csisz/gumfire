# M18 — Audio and art

Status: **done** (2026-10-06)

## Checklist
- [x] **Three new map themes** from the concept boards, painted with Higgsfield (text prompts,
      original designs, 18 images): **Toy Desk** (cardboard, notebook paper, pink eraser and
      toy-block wood under a torn-paper top; ink below), **Garage Junkyard** (packed dirt with
      bolts, riveted steel, tyre rubber, crate planks; motor oil below), **Bathroom Bubble
      Harbour** (yellow and green sponges, pink soap, tiles under bath foam; bath water below).
      Each has a painted backdrop, set dressing (crayon, block, paint pot, sharpener / nut,
      spring, traffic cone, oil can / rubber duck, soap pump, toothbrushes, bubbles) and its own
      air (dust motes, rising soap bubbles).
- [x] **Theme registry** (`world/themes.ts`): terrain chunks, crusts, liquid, backdrop, props,
      air, preview colours and music in one place; theme menus are built from it; replays and
      online matches carry the theme id.
- [x] The art pack loads **per theme** (only what the chosen map needs, cached), instead of all
      ~13 MB at once.
- [x] **Sound engine**: effects and music buses, a generated room reverb, a limiter, stereo
      placement by screen position, small random pitch so repeats stay alive, layered blasts
      (crack, body, rumble, crystal fizz), bubbly splashes.
- [x] **Gummy voices** (formant-filtered gibberish): "ouch" when hurt, "oof" on a hard landing,
      "bye-bye" going under, a cheer for the winners.
- [x] **Music**: a procedural tune per theme and for the menus (glockenspiel for the frozen
      factory, ukulele-ish plucks at the picnic, marimba on the toy desk, muted guitar and funk
      bass in the garage, bubbly synth in the bathroom, chiptune menu). It gets busier in the
      last 10 s of a turn and turns minor with a low drone in sudden death; a fanfare when a
      match is won.
- [x] Options: separate **music volume**.
