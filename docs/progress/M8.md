# M8 — Vertical slice (GATE)

Status: **built — playtest round 1 done, tuned** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
One map, two teams, three weapons, wind, water, turn timer, win and rematch — playable
hot-seat in the real client, in the art direction of Viktor's reference boards.

## Deviations from the plan
- HUD is plain DOM + CSS, not React: the slice needs a handful of cards; React can come with the
  menus at M14 if they need it.
- "Determinism across worker": the full-match golden covers replay determinism; running the sim
  inside a worker is exercised when the server runs it at M17.
- Fonts (Fredoka, Patrick Hand) load from Google Fonts with system fallbacks; final faces: M14.

## GATE — slice playtest (§23)
Two people who know classic artillery games play a few hot-seat matches and say it "feels
right", or their feedback becomes tuning tasks before M9.

## Playtest round 1 (Viktor, 2026-09-30) → tuning
| Feedback | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Rolling Pin hits feel too soft for 30 damage — the target should fly | impulse 10 → 15 px/tick, upward share 0.19 → 0.25 (a level hit throws ≈ 160 px) |
| Backflip is a small hop back; should travel further | backflip vx −1.1 → −2.3 px/tick (≈ 165 px back on flat ground, same height, still no fall damage) |
| Name/hp labels overlap when Gumlings stand close | labels lift one row per overlapping neighbour |
| Background labels of two parallax layers clash | mid layer has no words; far layer paler |
| Sounds need work (later: recorded/pro SFX) | noted for M18 |
