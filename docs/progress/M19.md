# M19 — Balance pass

Status: **done** (2026-10-06).

## Tools
- `tools/ai/weaponLab.ts` — every attack weapon fired with the real sim at a lone target on
  flat ground, 16 / 60 / 180 / 340 / 520 px away, over a lattice of aims, charges and fuses.
  Shows what each weapon *can* do (best damage) and how forgiving it is (share of shots doing
  ≥ 25), independent of how well the CPU plays. About a minute.
- `tools/ai/balance.ts` — CPU-vs-CPU matches on generated maps with per-weapon statistics
  (picks, damage to enemies and own side per use, kills, hit rate), match length, first-mover
  wins, deaths by cause, crates. `node tools/bench/run.mjs tools/ai/balance.ts 10 normal`.

## Findings → changes
| Finding | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Popcorn Bomb: a 3-ammo weapon that kills from full health and is *more* forgiving than the rocket at range (38 % of shots ≥ 25 at 180 px) | bomblets 20 → 12 damage, spread 110° → 130° (now 10 % at 180 px; a perfect throw still can kill) |
| Sprinkle Drop: one use, two-turn wait, but only one bomblet ever hits (29 max) | spacing 30 → 22, bomblets 25 → 30 damage, radius 26 → 30 (58) |
| Frosting Blaster: 2 ammo, ~30 max, rarely useful | pop 15 → 20, flames 10 → 12, burn 3 → 4 per pulse |
| Boomerang Trowel: hard to use, 25 max | 25 → 30 |
| Magnet Bomb: ~60 % of all shots hit hard at every range (homing) — strongest per use for the CPUs | 45 → 40 damage |
| CPU-vs-CPU matches in caverns ran 300+ turns: walled-off teams plink rockets forever, and the round clock (which only counts active turn time) barely moves with fast CPU turns | ruleset `roundTurns`: sudden death also after a turn limit; the client sets 8 turns per Gumling (64 for 2 × 4). HUD warns "sudden death in N turns" for the last 10 |
| Normal CPU picked bouncing grenades that only work at one exact charge, then wobbled its aim (8 % hit) | levels that wobble now score their top 3 shots with the wobble applied (aim ± noise, charge ± noise) and prefer the ones that still work |

Unchanged on purpose: rocket vs grenade (50 vs 45, the rocket pays with wind), Acorn Mortar,
Jawbreaker, Toffee, Pomegranate (crate only), Cherry Bomb (drop-and-run), melee (30), crate
rates. No first-mover advantage (normal 7–11, hard 5–6 over the runs). The Cookie Roller is a
remote weapon (the lab does not detonate it) and Spatula Shove does knockback only.
