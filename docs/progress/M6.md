# M6 — Explosions, damage, knockback

Status: **done** (2026-09-30)

## Objective
Explosions that hurt: falloff damage, knockback that throws Gumlings (into water, off cliffs),
damage revealed only once the world settles, deaths with a death blast and chain reactions.

## Checklist
- [x] `explosions/explosion.ts`: `Explosion` record, pure `blastEffect` — falloff measured from
      the blast to the edge of the target disc (φ = 1 − d/R), damage = round(D·φ), impulse
      K·0.16·D·φ px/tick along (target − blast) biased upward by 0.35 r; `blastCharacters`,
      `blastBodies`; `DEATH_BLAST` (r 20, damage 10, knockback 1)
- [x] Explosion queue `state.pendingExplosions`: projectiles and death blasts enqueue, the
      queue resolves once per tick after projectiles (a batch snapshot, so chains land on the
      next tick); `ProjectileImpact` (hit / character) is now separate from `Exploded`
      (x, y, radius, damage, cause weapon|death, source)
- [x] Knocked characters go airborne, lose their charge, and are immune to fall damage until
      they land
- [x] `pendingDamage` + settle-then-reveal (ADR-006): `worldInMotion`, `quietTicks`,
      `SETTLE_TICKS` = 10, `revealDamage` (id order), `autoReveal` switch for M7's turn flow;
      fall damage also goes through pending
- [x] Death at 0 hp (`CharacterDied(reason: hp)`) with a death blast; drowning discards pending
      damage
- [x] `debugExplode` command (sandbox tool, tests); GameState schema 6
- [x] Sandbox: Z blast tool, white hit-flash, pending-damage counter, reveal pop-ups with the
      HP label counting down, confetti death burst, lollipop grave, screen shake, HUD
      (pending / settled / revealed / deaths)

## Automated tests (149)
New in M6: falloff (centre = full, half-way = half, edge = 0, monotone); impulse away from the
blast with upward lift, mirrored, ≈ 8 px/tick point-blank for D50 K1, knockback scales the push
not the damage; pending until settled then revealed ≥ 10 ticks after the last motion as one
number, no fall damage from the throw; two blasts add up; held reveal (`autoReveal` off) +
explicit reveal; death at 0 hp with a death blast chaining into a neighbour (both die, in
order); knocked into the water drowns without a reveal; loose bodies pushed; a direct rocket
hit costs ≥ 40 and spares the shooter; point-blank wall shot hurts the shooter; determinism.

## Manual test (headless Chromium)
Three Gumlings on slice-island; blasts showed the flash and pending counters, the reveal popped
−21 / −32 once everything settled, repeated blasts killed all three (confetti, graves, craters),
Verify passed (1440 ticks, 14 commands).

- Barrels, mines and crates joining the chain: M9–M10.
